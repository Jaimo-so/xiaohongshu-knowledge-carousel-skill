# Content integrity

## Purpose

Convert a source into carousel copy without deleting or collapsing its knowledge. Visual brevity must come from page structure and deliberate line breaks, not from removing definitions or meaning.

## Atomic knowledge inventory

Record every source point under all applicable categories:

- exact term and definition;
- expanded name, translation, or notation;
- meaning and practical significance;
- problem or need it addresses;
- causes and prerequisites;
- mechanism and causal relationship;
- ordered process steps;
- data, source, or input requirements;
- assumptions and conditions;
- outputs and expected effects;
- limitations and non-guarantees;
- failure sources and misleading interpretations;
- security, privacy, or permission constraints;
- verification, human review, or escalation requirements;
- suitable and unsuitable scenarios;
- examples, counterexamples, and exceptions;
- final operational checklist.

Do not merge categories merely because two items fit in one sentence. For example, “retrieval finds semantically related content” and “semantic relation does not guarantee factual correctness” are separate knowledge points and both must appear.

## Coverage matrix

Build a table before writing final copy:

| ID | Category | Source statement | Meaning that must remain | Page | Final literal copy | Visual evidence | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| K01 | Definition | … | … | 02 | … | … | Planned |

Use one row per atomic knowledge point. A row is complete only when both final copy and a supporting visual or deliberate text-only treatment exist.

## Page-density rules

- Give the exact definition its own visible location.
- Keep the “why” separate from the “what.”
- Give each complex process stage its own page or labeled card.
- Keep caveats adjacent to the claim they constrain.
- Put underlying technical preparation on a separate page when it contains multiple definitions.
- Keep failure sources and the user checklist in the final recap even if earlier pages mention them.
- Add pages if a complete definition would otherwise become tiny, vague, or absent.

## Copy review

Compare the final manifest against the coverage matrix line by line. Reject the copy if:

- a technical term remains undefined;
- a claim loses its boundary or non-guarantee;
- a mechanism is replaced by a slogan;
- several distinct steps become “the system processes it”;
- security or permission constraints disappear;
- human verification requirements disappear;
- the final recap contradicts or oversimplifies earlier pages.
